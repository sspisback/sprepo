FICTIONAL COMPANY: NORTHSTAR WORKS
Report period: Q3 2026

EXECUTIVE OVERVIEW
Northstar Works' Q3 revenue was £8.2 million, down 18% from £10.0 million in Q2. The primary cause was that two expected renewals moved into Q4 after customer procurement reviews took longer than planned. These renewals represent £1.1 million of revenue.

Q3 REVENUE DETAIL
The EMEA advisory line generated £4.6 million, £0.4 million below plan. The decline followed a reduction in short-term transformation engagements after three clients delayed project starts. The team expects £0.2 million of this work to begin in Q4, but the remainder is not confirmed.

The Northstar Analytics product generated £2.1 million, down £0.3 million quarter over quarter. The report attributes the change to lower one-time implementation fees. Subscription revenue increased by 6% and renewal rates remained at 92%.

The remaining £1.5 million came from other services and licensing, broadly in line with Q2.

RISKS AND ACTIONS
The £1.1 million delayed renewals are not lost, but the report says their Q4 close dates remain subject to customer approval. The commercial team will review both renewal plans by 15 October 2026. No other specific cause for the 18% decline is identified in this report.
